# Product FAQ

## What is Acme Cloud?
Acme Cloud is a managed storage platform with end-to-end encryption.

## Pricing
The Starter plan costs $9 per month for 100 GB. The Pro plan costs $29 per month
for 1 TB and includes priority support.

## Support Hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC. Pro customers get 24/7 chat.
