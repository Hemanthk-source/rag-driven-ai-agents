# Acme Corp Handbook

## Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave
up to 10 days can be carried over to the next calendar year.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00
in the employee's local time zone.

## Expenses
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days. Meals are reimbursed up to
$40 per day while travelling.
